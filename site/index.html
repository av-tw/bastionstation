What is Space Station 13?

A round-based roleplaying game on a space station that is trying to kill you. You get a job, a jumpsuit and about an hour. Bastion is a fork of MonkeStation 2.0, itself built on /tg/station, with a slower, medium-roleplay crowd and admins who answer ahelps.
